Postfix conversion

Precedence: * + ? (3) > · concatenation (2) > | union (1). Unary postfix operators go straight to the output; binary operators wait on the operator stack until something of equal or higher precedence must be popped first.

Each token from the postfix sequence pops fragments off a stack and pushes a new, larger fragment — exactly like evaluating a postfix arithmetic expression, except the "values" are little automata.
